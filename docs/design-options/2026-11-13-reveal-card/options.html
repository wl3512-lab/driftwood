DRIFTWOOD V2 · DESIGN OPTIONS · 13 NOV 2026

The end of a level is a flaw card you collect

After each level the helper's flaw becomes a trading card. It flips in, the stars slam on, and it gets stamped: BUSTED when you caught the helper, IT FOOLED YOU when you didn't. Tap the card to flip it for where you meet this in real life and what happened this time. "Add to field guide" flies it into your guide. The flaw name uses the chosen title lettering: Option B's heavy blocks, built in cell by cell like Option A. Copy comes from the level files.

01 · END-OF-LEVEL CARD

Two tables for the same card

Same card, same timing, same words. Only the setting changes. Click or press Enter on a card to flip it. Tab reaches the card, then the button.

ACRT table

The cut to the dark CRT stays, as it is now. The card is drawn in phosphor on glass, lying on a green grid table. Caught cards get a holo sweep. Fooled cards go grey with light cracks.

BOver the garden

No hard cut. The garden you just played stays behind, dimmed (healthy plants if you caught it, damaged ones if not). The card is a parchment card with an ink outline and hard shadow, like the seed packets. The field guide is a leather binder.

Timing (about 2.4 s, both options; the button works from the start and skips to the end)

  1. 0.15 s card drops and turns in from edge-on (0.65 s)
  2. 0.6 s flaw name builds in cell by cell, left to right
  3. 1.0 s stars slam on one per 0.25 s, the card dips on each; empty slots stay outlined
  4. 1.8 s fooled: cracks run across the card. Then the stamp slams and the card shakes
  5. 2.1 s caught: holo sweep. Field guide and button are already in place; Level 4 shows Biscuit's line
  6. Add to field guide: the card lifts, flies into its slot (0.75 s), the guide bumps, the button becomes Continue
  7. Reduced motion: everything fades in place, flip is a crossfade, the card fades into its slot

RECOMMENDATION

  • B, over the garden. It feels like the same game as the wooden seed shelf and the level cards, and the dimmed garden shows what your choice did without any words.
  • B's caught and fooled cards differ most at a glance: a bright holo card with a red stamp against a grey cracked one.
  • Pick A only if you want to keep the hard cut to the dark CRT. The card works in both.
