DRIFTWOOD V2 · DESIGN OPTIONS · 13 NOV 2026

Seed-packet almanac, opening cutscene, shape type

Three things Lucy asked for, two options each, at laptop (1280×800) and phone landscape (844×390). Everything is clickable: tap a packet to flip it, replay the cutscenes, try a locked packet. Nothing here touches the game yet.

01 · ALMANAC

Big seed packets that flip over

One packet per plant across all five levels (11 in all). Packets from levels you haven't reached are silhouettes stamped with their level. Tap an open packet: it lifts, flips (rotateY, 0.44 s) and the back shows how to grow it, its growth stages and the level's fact card. With reduced motion it crossfades instead.

AWooden seed shelfCHOSEN

The parchment world: packets stand on two shelves, one per half of the game. Reads as a collection you are filling in.

BInside the CRT almanac

Same packets, drawn in phosphor on the existing almanac tablet's glass. A SEEDS tab sits next to FACT CARDS on the bezel.

CHOSEN BY LUCY: A, THE WOODEN SHELF

  • The packets read as real objects you collect, which is what makes the unlock-per-level feel like progress; on the CRT they look like menu tiles.
  • Keep B's idea of the back: the fact card quote on every packet ties the shelf to the "check the almanac" habit.
  • Phone landscape fits all 11 packets without scrolling in A; B loses a row of height to the tablet bezel and the packets get small.

02 · OPENING CUTSCENE

Warn the player before Level 1

About 12 seconds, five beats, Skip in the corner, ends on a Start button. Both options play the same script (the BEATS array at the bottom of this file), so Lucy can rewrite a line or a duration once and both update.

The pets pop in, say hi, then Mango's bubble glitches into a wrong fact. The radish is in no level, so it spoils nothing. The end title is the chosen title screen (Type B): CRT black with scanlines, heavy block letters, cell-by-cell build-in.

03 · SHAPE TYPE

      
Letters drawn from shapes, not a font

      
Both are drawn in SVG from one 5×7 letter grid defined in this file (A–Z, 0–9, a few marks), so any word can be set. A keeps each pixel as its own square (columns doubled for weight) and builds in cell by cell. B fuses the cells into heavy solid letters (each column doubled) with a stepped shadow, closest to the screenshots.
